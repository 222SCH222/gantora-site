Gantora

MS Project schedules on your phone

Open Microsoft Project (.mpp, .xml) files on Android, read and edit the Gantt chart on site, track progress with a progress line and weekly % complete, auto-schedule with your country’s public holidays, see the weather forecast for the site, and export PDF or MS Project files. 15 languages.

Optional: ask Claude about your schedule with your own Anthropic API key.
