Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Gantora Android app (“the app”).

Who we are

Gantora is published by MB KJOS PROJECTS, company code 306920777, registered in Lithuania. Contact: sch@kjosprojects.com.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.
  • Your schedule files are opened and edited on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app only contacts other services when you use a feature that needs them, listed below.

Information kept on your phone

Files you open, your settings (language, holiday country, weather places, display options), translated task names and, if you add one, your Anthropic API key are stored only in the app’s storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Files you save or share go where you choose in the Android share sheet.

Services the app contacts

Weather – MET Norway (api.met.no). To show the forecast the app sends the coordinates of the place you picked, rounded to about 100 m. This is not your phone’s location: the app does not use GPS or location permissions. Forecast data © The Norwegian Meteorological Institute, licensed under CC BY 4.0. MET Norway privacy.

Place search – OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to find matching places. © OpenStreetMap contributors. OSM Foundation privacy policy.

Ask Claude and online translation – Anthropic (api.anthropic.com). Only if you add your own Anthropic API key. When you ask a question or translate with the key, the question and the schedule data needed to answer it (for example task names, dates, links and notes) are sent to Anthropic under your own Anthropic account and its terms. Anthropic privacy policy. Without a key nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Offline translation – Google ML Kit. The first time you translate into a language, the app downloads that language model from Google. Translation itself then runs on your phone; your task names are not sent. Google ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic information (device model, app and library version, performance and error data) to keep the service working. ML Kit terms.

Google Play. Buying and installing the app is handled by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy. We receive only the usual anonymous sales and crash statistics that Google Play shows to developers.

Like any internet request, these services see your IP address when the app contacts them. All connections use HTTPS.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your information, build profiles, track you across apps, or show ads.

Children

The app is a professional planning tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in Android settings. You can write to us at the address above with any question, and you may complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
